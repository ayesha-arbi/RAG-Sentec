### Q: What are the attendance requirements?

**Answer:** Candidates registered during a semester must maintain at least 75% attendance in each course or an aggregate attendance of at least 75% of the total classes held across all registered courses to be allowed to appear in university examinations. Students must also have at least 75% attendance at the Courses, Laboratory, and Practical classes to be eligible to sit for the University Examination. 

(Source: pp. 98, 117-119)

### Q: How is my GPA calculated?

**Answer:** Grade Point Average (GPA) for any semester shall be calculated as under:

$$\text{CPA / CGPA} = \frac{\sum (\text{credit hours of a course} \times \text{grade point})}{\text{Total Credit hours of the courses}}$$

### Q: I’m panicking because I think I’ve ruined my semester. What should I do?

**Answer:** Any student who fails to obtain at least 2.00 CGPA in First Year Fall Semester shall remain on probation during First Year Spring Semester, and during this period will be allowed to register for regular and backlog courses within the maximum limit defined in clause 6.5.3(d). Students obtaining less than 2.00 CGPA after being in probation shall not be allowed to register in subsequent semesters and will remain in probation, and must improve their CGPA up to 2.00 before being allowed to register in subsequent semesters. 

Additionally, a student may be allowed to withdraw from a course up to the 8th week of the semester (though the fee will not be refunded/adjusted and the transcript will record 'W'), but no request for withdrawal is accepted after the 8th week, resulting in a 'WU' grade in case of non-appearance in the examination.

(Source: pp. 93-94)
