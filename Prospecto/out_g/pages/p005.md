## VISION

*"Be a leader in enabling Pakistan's social and economic transformation"*

## MISSION

*"Acquire education and research excellence in engineering and allied disciplines to produce leadership and enabling application of knowledge and skills for the benefit of the society with integrity and wisdom"*

## QUALITY POLICY

NED University of Engineering and Technology believes in establishing environment conducive to continual improvement in its efforts for providing the highest level of quality education.

This University is making all out efforts to raise its standards through teaching excellence and quality research. These efforts are carried out with involvement of the entire University work force to obtain utmost possible satisfaction of its customers.

Our endeavours are to make our students useful to society in particular and to humanity in general. In dealings with industry and utilities, attempts are made to maintain standards of integrity as well as quality.
